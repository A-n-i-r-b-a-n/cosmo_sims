This is a simulation that implements the interaction of dark matter and baryons in an evolving 
universe where gas cooling and star formation physcis have been allowed. This simulation follows 
the evolution of the universe from redshift z = 99 to z = 0. While dark matter only interacts 
through gravity, baryons follow hydrodynamics, while some fraction of gas has been allowed to
cool down and form stars according to knwon physical laws.

===============
Color code
===============

Red :   Dark Matter
White:  Baryons 
Yellow: Star clusters
